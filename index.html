Footstep Power Generation

Renewable Energy Technology

Footstep Power Generation is a renewable energy technology that converts the mechanical energy produced by human footsteps into electrical energy using piezoelectric sensors. When people walk on the platform, pressure applied on the sensors generates electricity, which is converted, regulated, and stored in batteries for applications like LED lighting, charging systems, and smart devices. This eco-friendly system is highly useful in crowded places such as railway stations, malls, airports, and public walkways where continuous movement can generate significant energy. The system includes components like piezoelectric sensors, rectifiers, voltage regulators, and batteries. It helps reduce dependency on conventional energy sources, supports sustainable development, and promotes green energy solutions. Although the power generated from a single footstep is small, advancements in technology are improving the efficiency and future applications of this system in smart cities and public infrastructure.

GAN AI

AI & Deep Learning

Generative Adversarial Network (GAN) is an advanced AI and Deep Learning model used to generate realistic data such as images, videos, audio, and text. It consists of two neural networks called the Generator and the Discriminator. The Generator creates fake data, while the Discriminator checks whether the data is real or fake. Through continuous competition and learning, the Generator improves its output to create highly realistic results. GANs are widely used in image generation, medical imaging, animation, gaming, voice synthesis, and deepfake technology. They help automate data creation and improve image quality, but they also require high computational power and can sometimes be misused. Overall, GANs are an important and powerful technology in modern Artificial Intelligence applications.
